Editorial Policy: Who Writes StripWorld and How We Source Facts

SW
Last updated: September 28, 2026
This page is the authoritative statement of how StripWorld is produced. It applies to every page on this site.

Consumer safety advice is only worth reading if you can check who wrote it and what their incentives are. This page exists so you can do exactly that. It sets out who publishes StripWorld, what we do when we make a claim, how this site earns money, what we deliberately refuse to do, and how to get a mistake corrected.

Who publishes this site

StripWorld is published by StripWorld Editorial, an independent consumer-education project covering the Stripchat platform, webcam streaming technology, interactive hardware protocols, and personal payment security.

We publish under a named editorial team rather than an invented individual. We do not publish a fictional author, and we do not attach a personal title such as "senior analyst" to anyone who cannot be verified. If we cannot stand behind a claim under a real, accountable name, we do not make the claim.

An earlier version of this site credited a named author with a professional job title. That attribution did not correspond to a verifiable person and has been removed. Correcting it is described under Corrections.

How we research and source what we write

Every factual claim on this site is written to be checkable. In practice that means we follow three rules:

  • Primary sources first. Claims about how a feature, promotion or billing system works are taken from the platform's own published terms, help pages and on-site interface, not from third-party summaries.
  • We say what is not verifiable. If a promotional quantity or pricing figure changes without notice, we describe how it works rather than promising a specific amount. Any figure we do state carries the date it was checked.
  • We separate mechanism from outcome. We will explain precisely how a system is built, but we will not tell you that following a step guarantees a result, because we cannot verify outcomes we do not control.

How this site makes money

Affiliate disclosure

StripWorld is funded entirely by affiliate commissions. When you follow a link on this site to a partner promotion and register, we may receive a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it does not change what you are shown.

This creates a real incentive, and it would be dishonest to pretend otherwise. Three rules exist to stop that incentive from distorting the content:

  • Commission never buys a ranking or a recommendation. A partner does not get a favourable review, and a negative finding does not get suppressed.
  • Scam warnings are never monetised. Pages describing token generators, fake APKs and card-balance scams carry no affiliate links. We do not earn from the scams we warn about, and we have no interest in anyone falling for one.
  • Our warnings are our own. We reached the conclusion that "free token hack" tools are scams because they are all built the same way and none of them can work. That conclusion would be identical without a single affiliate link on this site.

What we will not publish

Several categories are permanently out of scope, regardless of commercial consideration:

  • Any tool, script, APK or generator claiming to create, generate or modify tokens, coin balances or card credits. No such tool can work, because balances are recorded server-side.
  • Any method for evading age verification, regional blocks or platform access controls.
  • Any claim of guaranteed earnings, guaranteed token amounts, or guaranteed traffic.
  • Any content involving minors, or any suggestion that a performer is under 18. We only ever discuss platform operation at a general level and never name individual performers.

Corrections

When something here turns out to be wrong, we correct it rather than quietly editing it. This most recent correction is the removal of the unverifiable author attribution described above.

For a correction to be made, a report should identify the page, quote the claim exactly as written, explain why it is inaccurate, and point to a source that contradicts it. Once verified, the page carries a visible note describing what changed and when, and its lastmod date in the sitemap is updated.

Scope and age

This site discusses adult-oriented platforms for an adult audience. It carries a visible 18+ / 21+ notice on every page, and we do not republish, link to, or describe any explicit material. Where a platform's terms vary by jurisdiction, we defer to those terms and to local law.

What this policy does not promise

Being transparent about sourcing, incentives and corrections makes a site worth reading. It does not make it a substitute for your own judgement, and it does not guarantee that a third party's offer, interface or terms will be what this site describes. Promotional offers change without notice. Always confirm current terms on the platform's own pages before acting on anything written here.
